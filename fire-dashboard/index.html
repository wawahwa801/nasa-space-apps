Fire safety dashboard

Enter cabin conditions and nearby fuels. They are compared with NASA microgravity fire experiments.

Prototype. Exploratory decision support only, not a certified safety assessment. Thresholds are editable reference values in js/parameters.js.

Inputs save automatically in this browser. Click a field to see what it is used for.

Live monitoring

Readings taken over time are compared to spot a fire starting or smoke building up. Record a reading every 5 minutes.

A reading saves the current CO, CO₂, O₂, temperature, humidity, H₂, CH₄ and pressure values.

    Readings (0)

    Each reading is compared with earlier ones. The rise thresholds are placeholders in js/parameters.js (THRESHOLDS.monitoring), not validated detection limits; tune them with real sensor data. The demo sequence is synthetic. CSV columns: a time column (date/time or minutes from start) plus any of CO, CO₂, O₂, temperature, humidity, H₂, CH₄, pressure.
