Evaluation on unseen experiments

Checkpoint selection uses validation groups. Test groups and identical recorded conditions are held out of training. The all-images retrieval library includes those held-out images; reported retrieval scores use training images only. Lower image errors are better.

Image preparation and source

Still-image IDs are joined directly to the NASA workbook. A fixed camera crop and equal-scale padding preserve relative flame sizes. One −5 EV exposure bracket is selected per row; source-marked saturated images are excluded. Masks are image-derived proxies. Methane is excluded because too few usable ignition groups remain in this subset.
