Evidence conditions and provenance

The retrieved image is a separate comparison. It is not ground truth for a new feature combination. SAFFIRE images use a documented low-light display transform; brightness is not heat flux.

150 image–feature pairs per source: BASS-II, SAFFIRE-I, SAFFIRE-III and SPICE (600 total). Without time, multiple frames share the same conditions, so the CNN estimates typical appearance rather than predicting a burn phase. Denser sampling is not more independent experiments. Green camera-illumination shots are excluded; filtered SAFFIRE sequences can have timing gaps.
Training & validation limits

Only BASS-B3 is independently held out in this small sample. SAFFIRE-I, SAFFIRE-III and SPICE have no independent validation trial here. A condition-responsive image is not proof of a verified physical effect.
