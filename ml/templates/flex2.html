What changes when I move the sliders?

Retrieval selects a recorded run nearest your pressure and temperature. Differences in ring size, brightness and colour can also reflect droplet size, oxygen, burn stage and camera settings.

The CNN changes its predicted pixels continuously. In a sweep across the decane training range, increasing pressure made its diffuse prediction brighter and larger; temperature caused smaller colour and shape changes. These are model responses, not validated physical effects. The heptane pressure range is narrow and its response is much smaller.

Temperature here is the chamber temperature, not the flame temperature. There is no supported rule here that hotter means redder, bluer or bigger.
