How this demo works

Each fueled room uses the droplet engine’s D² burn curve as a finite fuel proxy. Open passages share a bounded part of the consumed proxy. Neighboring rooms ignite when accumulated exposure reaches the chosen threshold; exposure cools over time. Changing parameters resets time and recomputes the full history.

22% O₂ / 78% N₂ is held fixed as your scenario assumption. K, transfer, cooling and ignition threshold are assumptions, not calibrated cabin measurements. Fuel, pressure and chamber temperature retain their recorded meanings; in assumed-K mode they do not automatically change the burn coefficient.

Engine provenance and model limits
Square rooms are graph nodes, not resolved geometry. No room temperature, smoke transport, oxygen depletion or ventilation field is calculated. FLEX-2 droplets do not validate spaceship compartment spread.
